# Privacy Policy for Polaris

Last Updated: June 11, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Polaris ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bot on Discord.

By using Polaris, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

Polaris collects and stores only the information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Server (Guild) IDs
* Experience (XP) and level data
* Cooldown timestamps
* Server-specific settings and configuration
* Level reward configuration

Polaris does not store message content for its leveling system.

## How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to provide and maintain the Bot's features, including:

* Tracking user XP and levels
* Managing level rewards and role synchronization
* Maintaining server-specific settings
* Preventing abuse through cooldown systems
* Providing leaderboards and progression features

## Data Sharing

Polaris does not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties.

Information may only be disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a governmental authority.

## Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Bot's services.

Server administrators may remove Polaris from their server at any time. Users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the developer.

## Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored data from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Third-Party Services

Polaris operates through Discord and relies on Discord's infrastructure and services. Users are also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the developer.

## Availability

Polaris is a private Discord bot and is not publicly advertised or distributed. The bot is currently operated only within specific Discord communities authorized by the developer.

Despite being a private bot, Polaris follows this Privacy Policy regarding the collection, storage, and handling of user data.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

Continued use of Polaris after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
